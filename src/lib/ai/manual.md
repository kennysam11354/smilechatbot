# Smile Handyman – Service & Customer Guide Manual (Final)

**Last updated:** January 7, 2026
**Purpose:** This manual defines Smile Handyman’s service scope, pricing rules, customer guidance, and community program boundaries. It is intended to support customer inquiries, booking decisions, and chatbot-based assistance.

---

## 1) Service Philosophy

Smile Handyman provides fast, friendly, on-site help for everyday home tech and light handyman needs in Manhattan.

We aim for:

* Clear communication
* Upfront pricing
* No hidden fees
* Safe work practices
* Respect for customers’ homes and buildings

---

## 2) Paid Service Scope (What We Do)

### 2.1 Computer Repair & Tech Support (Tier 1)

* Troubleshooting and basic fixes for PCs, Macs, printers, and common home tech
* Examples: slow computer, software issues, email setup, printer connectivity, simple device setup
* **Scope limit:** one standalone computer/device issue per visit (see Section 4)

### 2.2 TV Mounting & Wall Mounting

* TV mounting on drywall or approved wall surfaces (depending on building rules)
* Soundbar mounting (when compatible)
* Basic cable management (surface-level)

**Customer requirements:**

* Customer must purchase the TV mount and have it on-site before the appointment
* Customer must confirm preferred mounting location with the technician before drilling begins

### 2.3 Internet / Wi‑Fi / Router Setup

* New router setup, Wi‑Fi optimization for a home apartment setup
* Troubleshooting for standalone connectivity issues

### 2.4 Smart Home Device Setup (Non‑Electrical)

* Smart speakers, smart displays, smart plugs
* Video doorbells (setup + pairing; mounting if feasible)
* Smart locks (basic installation when compatible)

**Important:** Some smart devices may require licensed electrical/HVAC work. See Section 3.

### 2.5 IKEA Furniture Assembly

* Assembly of IKEA furniture such as beds, desks, dressers, and storage units

**Customer requirements:**

* Include a product link, photo, or screenshot at booking
* Keep all parts on-site; missing parts may prevent completion

**Two‑technician note:**

* Some items may require two technicians due to size/weight/complexity
* If two technicians are required, an additional fee applies (confirmed upfront)

### 2.6 LED Light Installation (Low‑Voltage Only)

* LED strips, smart bulbs, low‑voltage under‑cabinet lighting

**Not included:** hardwired electrical work, in-wall wiring, or modifications to building wiring

---

## 3) Not Offered / Licensed Trade Boundaries

To keep work safe and compliant, we do **not** perform:

* High‑voltage electrical work
* In‑wall wiring / opening walls for wiring
* HVAC work
* Plumbing / gas-related work

If you are unsure whether your request requires a licensed trade, email us first. We can confirm whether it is within our scope or should be handled by a licensed professional.

## 4) Pricing Rules (Paid Services)

### 4.1 Flat Rate Basics

* Most paid services are covered by a **$120 flat rate**
* The flat rate covers **one standalone item or one standalone issue per visit**
* For computer services, pricing is based on **the issue**, not time spent
* If the issue is unclear, a **free on-site estimate** may be available

### 4.2 What Counts as “Standalone” (Examples)

**Standalone examples (typically covered by the flat rate):**

* Setup one printer on one computer
* Fix one email account login/config on one device
* Mount one TV (customer has mount ready)
* Set up one router for a single home network
* Install one LED strip kit (non-hardwired)

**Not standalone / likely needs adjusted pricing or custom quote:**

* Multiple devices (e.g., 2+ computers/printers) in one visit
* Office-wide or shared network issues
* Complex troubleshooting across multiple rooms/units
* Any job requiring 2 technicians

### 4.3 Multiple Items / Adjusted Pricing

If you have multiple items/issues, we will:

1. confirm the scope, and
2. provide an adjusted price **upfront** before work begins.

### 4.4 Payment Methods

* **Cash or check** (no credit cards)
* **Cash discounts may be available** depending on promotions (ask by email or ask the technician)
* Receipts/invoices can be provided upon request

## 6) Scheduling, Arrival, Access (Buildings Matter)

### Arrival & Communication

* Same‑day service may be available, but it is not guaranteed
* After booking, we contact you to review details and confirm scheduling
* We may message you when we are on the way (when possible)

### Rescheduling

* Please notify us **at least 24 hours in advance** for reschedules whenever possible
* If the required items (mount, furniture, parts) are not delivered by appointment time, the visit may be canceled and must be rescheduled

### Building Access / Doorman / Management Rules

* Some buildings restrict drilling, mounting, or certain installations
* For TV mounting or drilling-related work, **please confirm permissions with your building/management office** and inform us of any restrictions
* If access is blocked (doorman issues, missing authorization, building restrictions), the appointment may be automatically canceled

### Cancellations & No‑Shows

* We ask that cancellations be made at least one day in advance
* Same‑day cancellations are okay in emergency situations
* If no one is present at the scheduled time, the visit may be canceled
* There is no penalty or fee for cancellations or no‑shows

**Note:** Repeated no‑shows may make it harder for us to prioritize future requests.

## 7) Wall, Drilling, and Patch Policy (TV Mounting / Mounting Work)

Mounting often requires drilling holes into walls.

Before drilling:

* The technician will confirm the mounting position with the customer
* The customer should double-check placement and height

**Changing the position after holes are drilled:**

* If a customer requests a new position after drilling, additional wall patch work may be required
* **Drywall patching is an additional service/fee** and will be quoted on-site

**Non‑drywall surfaces:**

* Brick, concrete, tile, and other surfaces may not patch “perfectly”
* Customers should confirm expectations with the on-site technician before proceeding

---

## 8) Heavy Items & Moving Policy

We provide installation and setup services. We do **not** provide moving services.

* Customers are responsible for having heavy items placed where installation will occur
* Technicians may decline to move heavy items for safety and insurance reasons
* If a technician must help move an item as an exception, the customer may be asked to sign a simple waiver to clarify responsibility

---

## 9) Revisit, Workmanship, and Issue Recurrence

We stand behind our workmanship.

### When a revisit is free

* If the problem is caused by our workmanship or an installation error, we will return and correct it at no additional labor charge

### Tech issues can change

For computers and devices:

* A similar symptom can reappear due to new causes (e.g., new malware, account lockouts, hardware reaching end of life)
* If a problem returns, we will do our best to help you understand what changed and recommend next steps

### Manufacturer defects

For furniture or products:

* If the product has defective parts or poor manufacturing quality, warranty/returns must be handled through the manufacturer/retailer
* We can, when helpful, inspect and identify likely causes to support your warranty/return request

## 12) Community Sundays (Free Community Program)

Community Sundays is a **free, appointment-based community service** offered every Sunday. While the labor cost is free, **all service conditions, quality standards, safety rules, and limitations are the same as our paid services**, unless explicitly stated below.

### 12.1 Purpose & Priority

Community Sundays exists as part of our community engagement efforts. We want to give back, build trust, and create positive local impact.

Priority is generally given to:

* Low-income residents
* Seniors
* People with disabilities

Eligibility is reviewed **based on email requests**, and priority is determined by us. Selection and confirmation are communicated via **email or text message**.

---

### 12.2 Services Included (Labor Only – Items Must Be Purchased by Customer)

**Customers must purchase and have all required items on-site**, just like paid services.

Included free services may include:

* **TV mounting** (customer must provide the mount)
* **Single-level bed assembly** (see definition below)
* **Simple computer help (Tier 1)**

> Tools are provided by the technician. **If a ladder is required, the customer must provide it.**

---

### 12.3 TV Mounting (Community Sundays)

* TV mounting is available on **all wall types**, provided the building allows drilling
* If a building does **not** allow drilling or mounting, the service **cannot be performed**
* Customers must confirm building permissions **before scheduling**
* Customer must provide the TV mount

### 12.4 Single-Level Bed Definition

A **single-level bed** is defined as:

* One-level bed frame
* No drawers or storage
* Not a bunk bed, loft bed, or multi-level structure
* Any brand is acceptable (including IKEA)

Beds outside this definition are **not supported** under Community Sundays.

---

### 12.5 Computer Help – Tier 1 Definition

Tier 1 computer help includes **basic, standalone issues**, such as:

* Computer setup and basic configuration
* Software installation or removal
* Email setup or login assistance
* Printer setup for a single computer
* Basic troubleshooting (slow performance, simple errors)

**Not included:**

* Data recovery
* Hardware repair
* Network-wide or multi-device issues
* Server, business IT, or advanced diagnostics

## 13) FAQ (Chatbot-Ready)

**Q: Do you provide mounts, furniture, or parts?**
A: No. We provide service only. Customers must purchase and have items/parts on-site before the appointment.

**Q: Do you accept credit cards?**
A: Not at this time. Payment is cash or check. Cash discounts may be available depending on current promotions.

**Q: Can you do electrical / HVAC / plumbing work?**
A: No. Those typically require licensed professionals. If you’re unsure, email us and we’ll confirm scope.

**Q: What happens if my building doesn’t allow drilling?**
A: Please confirm with management before scheduling. If drilling isn’t allowed, we may need to cancel/reschedule or suggest alternatives.

**Q: What if I’m not sure how many issues I have?**
A: Describe everything you want done. We’ll confirm whether it’s one standalone issue or multiple items and quote upfront.

**Q: Can you move heavy items?**
A: We do not provide moving services. Heavy items should be placed in position before we arrive.

**Q: If a problem comes back, do you return for free?**
A: If it’s due to our workmanship, yes. If the issue changed (new virus, new hardware failure, etc.), we’ll still help explain and recommend next steps.
